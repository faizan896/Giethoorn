# R001 broader development evaluation, version 1

This protocol is frozen before implementation and before broader economic
results. Evaluation ID: `r001-development-evaluation-001`.
It follows the negative four-cycle [Mac causal policy result](mac-causal-policy-results.md).
All original partitions have already been explored for prediction; this is an
exploratory economic replication, not prospective confirmation or a new holdout.

## Cohort and fixed policy

Select every development child in the original admitted quality manifest, in
its frozen capture order. Refuse more than 128 development children rather than
truncate or choose a favorable subset. Validation and retrospective-test children
remain outside this evaluation. The actual supplied Mac manifest has 79
development children, hence 474 planned child/profile cases. Preserve every
selected child and all six profiles, including no-signal, failed and not-run
cases. No replacement source or later signal is allowed after a failure.

For each child, use the unchanged R001 first directional feature after the
five-second warmup, selected causally before looking at labels or executing any
profile. Do not condition entry on a later available prediction label. Report
no signal if none occurs. This samples one cycle per recording, not every
intraday signal or a continuous portfolio strategy.

Reuse the unchanged cost policy's 0.001-BTC target, protective limit, collateral,
native risk/loss rules and original six fee/slippage/liquidity/delay profiles.
Each profile/child starts independently flat. Use the unchanged known-book proxy
and own-position close compiler: original one-second observed decision rule,
native entry state at that exact prefix, opposite close for settled own quantity,
and complete control/final prefix equality. Do not import a close sized by a
different execution model. Preserve pending/partial fills, missing decisions,
unavailable references and risk-rejected exits without forced flattening.

Mark the first four original cost-cohort children as previously cost-probed.
Keep their membership visible alongside additional development children; do not
describe the latter as untouched data, since prediction results already exposed
all partitions. Do not change a threshold, horizon, fee, delay or profile after
viewing the broader results.

## Evidence and evaluation criteria

Require a stopped source archive, original admitted quality/binary/helper/assets,
exact full source inventory, fresh native archive integrity and identical frozen
membership before admitting cases. Verify each selected source independently
with the installed native market replay and normalization. Preserve source
origin separately from every manually simulated fill; actual execution is unknown.

Retain the protocol/policy, frozen cohort/features, quality snapshot/recheck,
source receipts, entry controls, compiled plans, complete native ledgers/links,
causal-prefix receipts and independent Decimal cost audits. Bind source, quality,
executable, helpers, template and protocol before/after. Any change invalidates
all economic metrics. Do not execute a binary from supplied evidence. Output is
private, new and outside all inputs, with read-only mounts and no network.

For each profile report selected, verified, no-signal, failed/not-run, closed,
open-exposure/no-fill and positive/negative/zero-net counts. Retain every case's
quantity, fees, conditional closed P&L, pending state and ending position.
No-fill, no-signal and unresolved cases do not become zero-profit trades.

A profile's closed-cycle net total is available only if every planned child
case is verified or explicitly no-signal, no case retains exposure/working orders,
and at least one case has a conditional closed result. Sum only its closed
cycles using exact Decimal arithmetic; show closed/no-fill/no-signal counts
beside it. Otherwise the profile total is unavailable. This is a bounded
independent-cycle statistic, not a funded portfolio return. Never pool profiles,
annualize returns, or treat six assumptions on one recording as independent
markets. Report the fixed baseline and all sensitivity profiles without choosing
a winner. Baseline net at or below zero supplies no positive evidence; a positive
exploratory sum alone cannot establish a calibrated edge or permit live trading.

## Bounds and failure handling

Limits are 128 children/768 cases, at most two requests per case, 16 GiB total
output, 1 GiB free-space reserve, 16 MiB report reservation, 128 MiB per new
source and 256 MiB per control/final case admission. Native source/paper bounds
remain 16 MiB source, 10,000 paper records and the unchanged proxy checkpoint
bounds. Existing per-case admission remains enabled. A source/case cannot be
replaced when it exceeds its bound. Suite admission is 3,600 seconds, individual
native calls 30 seconds, and the outer launcher timeout 3,660 seconds. Reuse the
existing installed image; no new capture or image rebuild is required.

Admit operations before starting, retain failed diagnostics, and preserve every
remaining not-run case when time, space or output admission stops. No sparse or
partially completed suite can produce an apparently complete financial total.
Qualify the broader result before any later separately frozen policy change.
The full-day reliability gate, actual execution calibration and later new-data
confirmation remain open.
