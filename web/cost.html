The scope of this test
A close uses only position information known at its recorded decision. A missing interval creates no clock events or fills. A risk halt can reject the close; unresolved positions stay open. Historical marks are not a current or executable valuation.
Each recording/profile starts flat with independent paper collateral. There is no combined portfolio return. This archive has already been explored; later confirmation needs separately frozen data after the policy is fixed. These results do not complete the 24-hour reliability gate or enable live orders.
