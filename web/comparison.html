Interpret the comparison
Actual execution is unknown for every row. A public quote can remain visible between messages without proving that its displayed size was available to a real order. The alternative is an assumed standing-book proxy, not measured order latency, a calibrated matching model or a new strategy backtest.
The original policy compiled its later requests from its original path. Those requests are held fixed here; they are not recomputed from the alternative position. Partial fills, rejected requests, pending orders and open exposure stay in the result. No forced closing price or portfolio total is invented.
All arrivals, acknowledgements, fills and cancellations in the alternative ledger are labelled manual simulation. A verified comparison establishes source/ledger/arithmetic consistency. The operational day gate, actual execution calibration and fresh-data economic confirmation remain open.
