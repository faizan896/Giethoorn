Read the experiment
This is a new causal policy run on already explored recordings. Each child/profile starts flat independently; the six profiles are not independent markets and their P&L is not summed into a portfolio return.
The unchanged known-book proxy assumes available size, arrival, adverse fill price and fees. Native replay verifies source, accounting and causal-prefix consistency. It does not establish actual order latency, fill probability or economic viability.
No close is invented when the entry is flat, still working or has no observed decision. Partial exits, risk/loss rejections and source-end pending orders remain in the result. No forced price, retry or risk-halt bypass is used to make the position flat. The operational day gate and actual execution calibration remain open.
