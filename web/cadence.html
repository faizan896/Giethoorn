Use this as a cadence measurement
These are intervals between recorded messages or fresh native opportunities, not order-channel network latency or a measured probability of filling a taker order. Quiet public data does not prove there was no standing executable liquidity.
Baseline [100,350) ms and delay-stress [250,500) ms windows remain the original assumptions. This report changes no deadline, signal, order, fee or historical P&L. A different future policy needs its own version and separately frozen evaluation data.
Repeated/older exchange times and unusable messages are retained as context. Source clock steps are flagged without deleting their intervals. Empty series have unavailable percentiles. These recordings are already explored; the full-day reliability gate and actual execution calibration remain open.
