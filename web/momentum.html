How to read this research
The hurdle is the current full spread plus 14 basis points: ten for assumed round-trip fees, two for adverse slippage and two as a buffer. Past movement is an entry filter, not an expected future return. Native accounting charges actual assumed fill costs once.
The first qualifying feature per recording is fixed before forward labels and fills. A rejected order, missing label or losing result never selects a replacement. The same explored development cohort and all six profiles remain visible.
A first close uses a fresh observed quote from 30 through 31 seconds after the feature and its known native position. At most one further close may use a settled residual. Missing, partial, pending and risk-rejected execution retain exposure; nothing forces a position flat.
All fills are manual simulation. Actual venue execution is unknown. Independent cases and assumption profiles are not pooled into portfolio returns. A passing screen warrants only separately predeclared new-data confirmation; execution calibration and production gates remain open.
