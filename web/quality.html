What qualifies as a candidate?
A finalized recording with usable BBO updates no more than 250 ms apart, lasting at least 1.25 seconds. A changed connection, unusable feed, repeated exchange timestamp or detected clock discontinuity breaks the span. No time is added after the last quote.
Feed freshness follows each recording’s original policy. The tighter research cadence is a separate check. State durations stop counting fresh time at its deadline; a gap with no recorded transition stays unobserved. These measurements describe saved events, not continuous exchange delivery or measured execution latency.
