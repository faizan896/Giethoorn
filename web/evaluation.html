What this evaluation can tell us
The signal, one-second horizon, target size, risk and six execution assumptions stay fixed. Every development recording is selected before running its first eligible signal; later labels and outcomes do not choose a replacement.
The first four cost-probed children stay labelled. All original partitions have already been explored for prediction. This is an exploratory economic replication of independent cycles, not a continuous portfolio backtest or a fresh holdout. Profiles are not pooled into a return or treated as independent markets.
All fills remain manual simulation. Actual venue execution is unknown. A positive exploratory result alone cannot establish a calibrated edge or enable live trading. The operational day gate, execution calibration and later new-data confirmation remain open.
