Read this as an exposure experiment
The same frozen development recordings, signal, first entry/close, costs, protective limit envelope and risk rules are used. The full cohort stays selected, including no-signal, no-fill, failed and not-run cases. All original partitions have already been explored.
A new accepted BBO after the first close’s source anchor is required. The first attempt must be terminal with no pending order or cancellation. Native risk halt stops a retry. Its quantity comes only from the position already known at the selected prefix; later prices or fills cannot change it.
The experiment adds at most one close. It can still fail to fill, close partly or remain pending at source end. Existing manual-ledger rules do not invent liquidity, prices, reduce-only support or an additional retry to make inventory flat.
All fills are manual simulation and actual venue execution is unknown. Independent profile starts are not pooled into portfolio returns. More closed cases do not establish an economic edge or make the current R001 policy ready for live trading. The day gate, execution calibration and new-data confirmation remain open.
