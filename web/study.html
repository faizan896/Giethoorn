    Rules fixed before outcomes
    Use the first eligible new BBO quote in each five-second window. Predict up if bid size is at least three times ask size, down for the reverse, otherwise abstain. The label is the first new BBO quote 1–1.25 seconds later in the same connection, with no update gap over 250 ms.
    Gaps, clock discontinuities, repeated exchange timestamps and unusable feeds make pending labels unavailable. Earlier features are never replaced after seeing an outcome. Exact per-sample price-change fractions are retained; aggregate basis-point values round each sample half-even to 12 decimal places before averaging.
    Counts are descriptive. Observations can be correlated; statistical significance and economic value have not been established. Source integrity and this computation are checked independently of collector uptime. A completed study does not complete the 24-hour reliability gate or authorize trading.
