What these windows mean
Baseline arrives at request +100 ms and expires at +350 ms. Delay stress arrives at +250 ms and expires at +500 ms. The shifted window can encounter a different quote. It does not measure network latency or establish that extra delay improves trading.
At exact expiry, rejection happens before trying that event’s quote. An interval with no recorded opportunity creates no invented timer, acknowledgement or fill. Stale and repeated quote states remain visible. Prices, quantities, position and historical results are preserved.
This diagnostic freshly checks native source normalization, the exact saved ledger, cost arithmetic and causal entry/close compilation. It generates no orders, re-estimates no profit and identifies no exchange/transport/Internet root cause from gaps alone. Source clock domains remain separate. The full-day reliability gate and execution calibration remain open.
